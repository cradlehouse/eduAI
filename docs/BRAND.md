# Imaje brand principles (draft, 2 Oct 2026)

What the brand stands for and how it should behave. **This page deliberately has no taglines, sample copy
or logo ideas**: those are the job of a brand identity designer, working from this page and
`BRAND_BRIEF.md`. Facts behind it: `BUSINESS.md`, `PRODUCTION_FLOW.md`, `DESIGN.md`,
`RESOURCE_NEUTRALITY.md`, and the claims research in `research/brand-claims-2026-10.md`.

## What Imaje is

A film school in the browser. Students write a script, build their cast and locations once, plan the
shots, choose the takes and cut the film. AI does the rendering; the student makes the director's
decisions.

## The problem the brand exists to solve

Two groups hold back from AI, and both have good reasons.

- **Young people.** Many in this generation avoid AI: it feels like taking from artists, it burns
  energy and water, it makes generic work, and it feels like cheating.
- **Schools and youth programmes.** AI means deepfakes of students, content nobody controls, surprise
  costs, and one bad incident costing a programme its funding.

Imaje has to win over both. It does that two ways:

1. **Protected.** Students are sheltered from the wildness of open AI tools.
2. **Principled.** Students build with the best tools without taking from other people's creative work
   or from the planet.

That's what we're selling. The filmmaking craft is how it's delivered.

## Principles

Each one has what it means in practice and what it rules out. Use them to judge any screen, deck, page or
partnership.

### 1. The student is the director
AI is the crew. Every creative decision belongs to the student, and so does the credit.
- **Means:** choices sit with the student; the film is always "theirs"; we show their work, not our tech.
- **Rules out:** "AI made this", one-click films, any feature that decides for them.

### 2. Protected by default
The safe path is the default path, not a setting someone has to find.
- **Means:** screened prompts, releases for any real person (a guardian's under 18), instructor
  oversight, prices before anything runs, nothing deleted.
- **Rules out:** safety as small print; making safety feel like a punishment or a lecture.

### 3. Make your own, take from no one
Students build their own characters, worlds and stories. We respect other people's creative work: in
what students make, and in which tools we choose.
- **Means:** originality is built into the flow; every model's training data is rated and visible;
  we move towards fairly trained models as they become good enough.
- **Rules out:** borrowing famous characters or living artists' styles as a feature; claiming the
  whole stack is "ethically trained" before it is.

### 4. Light footprint, honestly counted
We take the environmental cost of AI seriously and show it as it is.
- **Means:** measure what we can, say "undisclosed" where we can't, choose cleaner compute where we
  control it, teach that a shorter take is a lighter take.
- **Rules out:** made-up numbers, offsets passed off as neutrality, "green" as decoration.

### 5. Say where we are
The promises are a direction, and we say plainly how far along we are. With this audience, an honest
"not yet" earns more trust than a polished claim that falls apart when a student looks it up.
- **Means:** every claim matches what's true today; gaps are named with what we're doing about them.
- **Rules out:** future tense dressed up as present tense.

### 6. Craft, not magic
Filmmaking is a discipline and we treat it like one. Real film terms, real steps, real choices.
- **Means:** script first, shot by shot; film vocabulary used properly and explained once.
- **Rules out:** "magic", "instant", "Hollywood in seconds"; anything that makes the work look effortless.

### 7. The work is the hero
The brand frames student films and gets out of the way.
- **Means:** student work up front (credited, with releases); a quiet, dark, set-like visual system
  where the images supply the colour (see `DESIGN.md`).
- **Rules out:** generic AI imagery (glowing brains, robots with cameras, purple gradients, sparkles),
  stock photos of kids at laptops.

### 8. Calm on set
Imaje behaves like a good crew member: calm, organised, a step ahead, never condescending.
- **Means:** plain words, sentence case, the cost shown as a number, problems explained in one line with
  what to do next.
- **Rules out:** hype, exclamation marks, childish tone, blame, jargon on top of film terms.

## Tensions to hold

These are where the brand will be hardest to get right. A good identity holds both sides at once.

| Pull one way | Pull the other way |
|---|---|
| Safe enough for a school and a parent | Not childish or institutional; teenagers have to want it |
| Principled about AI | Not preachy, and not anti-AI either: we use the best tools |
| Built for AI sceptics | Still exciting to make things with |
| A real film set, serious craft | Approachable on day one for a 13-year-old |
| Honest about gaps | Confident, not apologetic |

## Who it has to work for

| Who | What wins them | What loses them |
|---|---|---|
| Programme lead (buyer) | Real film education, predictable cost, nothing goes wrong in front of funders | Any hint of risk to students or reputation |
| Instructor | Sees every step, sets the budget, class doesn't stall | Chaos; outages in a lesson |
| Student (often under 18) | Their own film, their own ideas, a tool they're not embarrassed to use | Feeling like a cheat, or part of something they think is wrong |
| Guardian | Nothing real of their child used without a signature | Deepfakes; data sold |
| Funder | Skills, access for under-served young people, a responsible AI stance | Anything that looks like a cheating, art-theft or carbon machine |

The programme lead, instructor and guardian care most about **protected**; the student and funder care
most about **principled**. Both have to be present for everyone.

## Hard rules

- No claim that isn't true today. Check `STATUS.md` and the claims research before anything goes public.
- No student faces or voices in marketing without a release that covers marketing use.
- No vendor logos or implied endorsements.
- Pegasus Media Project is the design partner; nothing goes out in their name without Niloo's sign-off.
  Their students are "apprentices" in their materials.
